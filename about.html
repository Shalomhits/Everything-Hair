OUR STORY

Beauty is personal.
So is your hair.

Everything Hair is built around the idea that your hair should help you express who you are—whether your style is classic, playful, bold or beautifully low-key.

WHY EVERYTHING HAIR

Options for the way you want to show up.

Founded in 2024, Everything Hair focuses on hair products for different styles and routines, including human hair bundles, braiding extensions, French curl hairpieces, frontal laces, closures and custom wigs.

We know choosing the right product can involve questions about the look, texture, length and care. Our goal is to make exploring the collection simple and give you a clear way to ask before you order.
