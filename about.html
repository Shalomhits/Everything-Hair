Get to Know Us

About Everything Hair

Everything Hair was created with a simple idea: finding quality hair should feel exciting, personal and accessible.

Founded in 2024, Everything Hair is a growing hair retail business offering a selection of hair products for customers who enjoy expressing themselves through their hairstyles.

Our Story

Everything Hair started from a passion for beautiful hairstyles and the confidence that comes with finding the right look. What began as a small business connecting with customers through social media and WhatsApp has grown into a brand with a vision for creating a more convenient shopping experience.

We understand that choosing hair can sometimes feel overwhelming. There are different textures, lengths, colours and styles to consider. Our goal is to make that process easier by bringing a variety of options together in one place.

As Everything Hair continues to grow, we want our website to become more than an online catalogue. It should be a place where customers can explore, discover inspiration and confidently choose products that suit their individual style.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with quality, versatile and affordable hair products while creating a friendly and convenient shopping experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted hair brand known for quality products, personal service and helping customers feel confident in every style they choose.
