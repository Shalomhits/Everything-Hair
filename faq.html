Need Some Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

We know that choosing hair can come with a lot of questions. Here are some of the questions customers may have before making an enquiry or placing an order.

Common Questions

What types of hair products does Everything Hair offer?

Everything Hair offers a range of hair products, including human hair bundles, synthetic braiding extensions, French curl hair, frontal laces, pre-plugged frontal closures and custom wigs.

How do I choose the right hair for me?

The right choice depends on the style you want, the occasion, your preferred texture and the type of look you are trying to achieve. If you are unsure, you can send us an enquiry and tell us what you have in mind.

Do you offer custom wigs?

Yes. Everything Hair offers custom wig options for customers who want a more personalised hairstyle. You can use our enquiry page to tell us what you are looking for.

Can hair stylists purchase products from Everything Hair?

Yes. Our products can be suitable for both individual customers and professional hair stylists. Stylists can contact us to discuss their specific requirements.

How can I enquire about a product?

You can use the Enquiry / Order page to select the product you are interested in and provide details about your preferred style, length, colour and quantity.

Can I contact Everything Hair through WhatsApp?

WhatsApp communication is part of the planned customer service experience. The official WhatsApp contact details will be provided once the business contact information has been confirmed.

How long does it take to receive an order?

Order and delivery times may depend on the product, availability and delivery location. Customers should confirm the expected timeframe when making an enquiry.

Do you accept returns or exchanges?

Return and exchange conditions depend on the final Everything Hair business policy. Customers should confirm the applicable policy before completing an order.

How should I care for my hair?

Hair care depends on the specific type of hair and style. Customers should follow the care instructions provided for their selected product and ask for guidance if they are unsure.

What if I cannot find the product I am looking for?

If you cannot find what you are looking for, contact us through the enquiry page. We can discuss your requirements and let you know what options may be available.
